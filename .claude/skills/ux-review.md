# UX Review

Evaluate the experience from the perspective of a real user.

Ask:

1. What is the user's goal?
2. Is the goal immediately obvious?
3. What is the primary action?
4. Is the action easy to discover?
5. Are there unnecessary steps?
6. Are there unnecessary decisions?
7. What happens when there is no data?
8. What happens when something fails?
9. Can the user recover easily?
10. Does the experience work well on mobile?

Prioritize low-friction daily interactions.
